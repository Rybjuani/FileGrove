item in it into a FileGrove requirement. In particular:

- Scheduled/cron mode is retired. Manual and Automatic are the product modes.
- Confirmed recursive Automatic organization is implemented and tested even
  though old sections of the specification describe it as incompatible.
- Licensing, trials, paywalls, usage limits, accounts, HWID, and telemetry must
  not be added.

- Windows and macOS remain future work; do not mix them into the Linux release.
- FileGrove's business/product target is capability parity with the competitor's
  free and Pro plans at no cost; do not add paid editions or usage paywalls.

## Completed product scope

Roadmap stages 0.2, 0.3, and 0.4 are complete:

- Durable, multi-session Undo with newest-first historical rollback.
- Cancellation preserves an Undo entry for every completed move.
- Safe rename/skip/overwrite conflicts and exact duplicate comparison.

- Managed destination folders generated inside each selected root.

- Clear dashboard progress and dedicated historical Undo feedback.

## Definition of Linux 1.0 ready

FileGrove is ready only when package validation and every roadmap 0.9 safety
scenario pass on the same audited commit; English and Spanish documentation is
complete; an ordinary upgrade preserves user data; no plausible file-loss issue
remains open; CI is green; artifacts and checksums are verified; and the owner
explicitly approves making the repository and release public.
